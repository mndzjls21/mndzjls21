<summary><strong>NUTRI MAP</strong> · Designing for the moment connectivity drops</summary>

**My role — Requirements Engineering & Systems Analysis**

During a community nutrition assessment, a field worker needs to finish recording measurements even if the phone loses signal. City nutrition staff have a different question later: which areas have reported, and where is information still missing?

For our thesis, formerly SNUHI, I mapped the Operation Timbang Plus workflow from field entry to reporting. I translated it into requirements for offline Android forms, measurement validation, encrypted synchronization, and role-based review. Those same reporting questions guided my planning for the GIS dashboard.

<details>
<summary><strong>StockWise & MarketMate</strong> · Turning a limited budget into a restock plan</summary>

**My role — Technical Leadership & Project Planning**

Give a seller a ₱2,000 restock budget and the first question is what to prioritize. A product that sells quickly can compete for that budget with one that earns more per sale.

I helped scope the Smart Restock Planner around three strategies — profit, volume, and balanced growth. I also mapped how a near-expiry alert could lead to a MarketMate promotion while the seller still has time to act.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Supply Chain Portal</strong> · Testing what happens after “Delivered”</summary>

**My role — Workflow Analysis & Quality Assurance**

A driver submits proof of delivery. Can the coordinator see it? Who receives the notification? Who has permission to complete final verification?

I traced those handoffs across sales coordination, dispatch, and driver workflows. I designed role-based test scenarios, documented defects, and tracked the checks that still needed retesting.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Platform Aggregator</strong> · Planning around third-party API limits</summary>

**My role — Technical Project Leadership & Systems Architecture**

When API restrictions limited what our team could test, I revised the integration plan. We used simulated sources to check how records from different platforms appeared in one interface. I documented which behaviors we had demonstrated and which still required live verification.

</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Local Café Management System</strong> · Tracing a sale from transaction to report</summary>

**My role — Database Architecture & Data Modeling**

I designed a normalized PostgreSQL schema for café sales and inventory. I defined the relationships, constraints, and custom types, then documented them in a data dictionary so another developer could follow a transaction from the original sale into an operational report.
