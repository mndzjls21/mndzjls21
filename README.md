<details>
<summary>🏥 <strong>Barangay Health Center</strong> · NUTRI MAP</summary>

**What happens to a field record when the internet drops halfway through a visit?**

For our community nutrition surveillance thesis, I mapped how a child’s growth assessment moves from a barangay health worker’s phone to a city health dashboard. My work focused on what the app needs to save offline, what it should validate in the field, and who can review a record after it syncs.

**My contribution** · Requirements engineering, business rules, offline workflows, and GIS dashboard planning.

</details>

<details>
<summary>🏪 <strong>Sari-Sari Store</strong> · StockWise & MarketMate</summary>

**If you had ₱2,000 to restock today, what would you buy first?**

I helped plan a restock tool around that question. A seller can prioritize profit, sales volume, or a balance of both. I also mapped how a near-expiry alert could lead directly to a MarketMate promotion, so the seller has an action to take while there’s still time to sell.

**My contribution** · Project scoping, inventory workflows, restock planning, and marketing automation.

</details>
